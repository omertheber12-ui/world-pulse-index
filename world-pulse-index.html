How the portfolio effect is scored

Input is Polymarket percentage movement, not news. The score updates when the figure moves, whether or not the event happens.

Touch = sum over holdings of (holding weight × link strength). High effect when touch is above 30%.

Uncertainty = 4 × p × (1 − p), where p is the leading option as a fraction from 0 to 1.

Attention = touch × uncertainty, plus touch × 0.5 when there is a meaningful move (5 points or more versus about 24 hours earlier, lasting at least one hour). Capped at touch.

Every event-to-holding link is labeled Guess. Strength numbers are labeled Assumption.

Rules file: rules.json (swap to replace Product's map). Doc: Product Rules · TP-010.
